# 3 Tips to Make a Tapping Game Fun on a Phone

For a game where you tap the screen again and again to score.

## 1. React right away

**Why:** If the game answers late, tapping feels mushy and players stop trusting it. When people tap fast, they need a fast answer.

**You'll see/feel:** You tap, and the score goes up and the button squishes in the very same moment. No waiting.

## 2. Make every tap pop

**Why:** Small, quick reactions tell the player "that worked!", and that feels good again and again. Keep them small, because too many effects turn into noise.

**You'll see/feel:** The button shrinks a little and bounces back, a soft "pop" plays, and a little "+1" floats up and fades. If the phone can, give a tiny buzz too. It's a bonus, because some phones can't buzz.

## 3. Make the tap area huge

**Why:** Fast fingers miss small buttons, and a miss feels unfair. If the whole screen counts, every tap lands.

**You'll see/feel:** You tap anywhere, even at the edge with your thumb, and it always scores. Keep the score text out of the way so you never hit it by accident.

---

**Good to know:** Phones only play sound after the player has touched the screen once, so let the "Start" tap turn the sound on. Test on a real phone, and try different bounce sizes until it feels right.
