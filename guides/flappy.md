# How to play Flappy

Flappy is a one-button game where you guide a little bird through gaps in the pipes.

- **Flap:** press **Space**, click the game, or tap the screen on a phone or tablet.
- **Score:** you get 1 point for every pipe you fly past. Touching a pipe or the ground ends the game.
- **Extras (work in every game):** **P** or **Esc** pauses, **M** turns sound on or off.

**Tip:** Each press gives one small hop, so use quick, gentle taps and keep the bird near the middle of the gap.
