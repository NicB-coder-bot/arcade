# How to play Snake

Snake is a classic game where you steer a growing snake around the board to eat apples.

- **Steer:** press the **arrow keys** or **W A S D** (W is up, A is left, S is down, D is right). On a phone or tablet, swipe in the direction you want to go.
- **Score:** you get 1 point for every apple you eat, and the snake gets longer and faster each time.
- **Game over:** you lose if you hit a wall or run into your own body.

**Tip:** You can press two turns quickly in a row and the game remembers them, so use that for tight U-turns. Plan ahead and stay away from the edges.
