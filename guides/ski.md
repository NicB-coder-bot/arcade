# How to play Ski

Ski is a downhill game where you race down a snowy slope and dodge trees, rocks and other skiers.

- **Steer:** hold the **left and right arrow keys** or **A and D** to move sideways. On a phone or tablet, touch the left half of the screen to go left and the right half to go right.
- **Score:** your score is how many meters you ski, and you go faster the longer you last, up to a top speed.
- **Game over:** you crash if you touch a tree, a rock or another skier.

**Tip:** Watch the bottom of the screen, because new obstacles appear there before they reach you. There is always a gap to slip through, and the other skiers drift side to side, so give them extra room.
